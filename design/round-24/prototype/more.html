Why Settings is not a bottom-bar tab

Bottom-bar slots are the most valuable space in the app and belong to the things a person opens while training. Settings is opened rarely and deliberately; putting it one tap deeper costs almost nothing and buys a calmer bar. Achievements sits beside it for the same reason: it is a reward to visit, not a daily destination.
